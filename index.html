Mysterious Creatures of the Night

Moths are fascinating insects that can be found throughout the world. Although they are often compared to butterflies, moths come in thousands of different shapes, colors, and sizes.

Many moths are active at night and are known for their unusual patterns and camouflage. Some species appear soft and colorful, while others have darker markings that give them a mysterious appearance.

Death's Head Hawkmoth

One of the most recognizable moths is the death's head hawkmoth. It is known for the skull like marking on its body and has become associated with horror, mystery, and gothic imagery.
